## A note on attribution

Argyph is built with substantial AI assistance, most often Claude. The architectural decisions, the cuts, the trade-offs, the implementation reviews, and the choice of what to ship are human work. The commit log reflects that: the named `Author:` of every commit is the human who reviewed and merged the change.

When AI assistance is materially load-bearing on a particular commit — typically a discrete feature implementation where the AI did the generative work and the human reviewed and integrated it — that commit may carry a `Co-authored-by: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` trailer. Routine assistance does not warrant a trailer. See [`docs/COMMIT_CONVENTIONS.md`](docs/COMMIT_CONVENTIONS.md) for the full policy.
